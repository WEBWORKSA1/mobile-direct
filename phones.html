How the scores work

How are phone scores calculated?

Each phone gets five sub-scores from published specs: performance from its chipset tier, camera from lens count, telephoto and sensor resolution, battery from capacity with an efficiency adjustment, display from size and refresh rate, and value from overall quality relative to launch price. Your weights decide how they combine.

Are these lab test results?

No. Scores are spec-based and meant for shortlisting. Read hands-on reviews before you buy.
