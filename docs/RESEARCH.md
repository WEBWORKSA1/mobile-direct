## Patterns that recur across the winners

1. **Tool first, content second.** Every top site leads with an interactive finder or calculator; editorial content supports it.
2. **Money pages are comparisons**, not single-product pages: plans vs plans, phone vs phone, buyer vs buyer.
3. **Trust devices**: "last updated" stamps, methodology pages, affiliate disclosures, verified badges.
4. **Lead capture is a questionnaire** that returns a personalised answer (Navi, Goji, WhistleOut Q1-of-3).
5. **Monetisation is stacked**: display ads + affiliate + lead gen + B2B data/partnerships.
6. **Segments**: seniors, students, families, travellers, newcomers, business.
