    eSIM compatibility checker

    The 10-second test for any phone

    Open the phone app and dial *#06#. If an EID (a 32-digit number) appears, your phone supports eSIM. The phone must also be unlocked to add an eSIM from another provider.

    Before you fly

    1. Confirm your phone is unlocked.
    2. Check the destination's bands with the band checker.
    3. Install the eSIM at home on Wi-Fi; leave it switched off.
    4. Turn off data roaming on your home line.
    5. Download offline maps as a backup.

    Read more: eSIM explained and roaming vs travel eSIM.

    Questions

    Do I need to be on Wi-Fi to install a travel eSIM?

    Yes, the profile downloads over the internet. Install it at home before you leave and switch it on when you land.

    Will I still receive calls on my normal number?

    Yes, if you keep your home line enabled. Incoming calls may cost roaming charges depending on your plan.

    What does fair use mean on unlimited eSIMs?

    Most unlimited travel plans slow your speed after a daily amount of high-speed data. Check the threshold on the provider's plan page.
