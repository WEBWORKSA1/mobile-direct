    eSIM compatibility checker

    The 10-second test for any phone

    Open the phone app and dial *#06#. If an EID (a 32-digit number) appears, your phone supports eSIM. The phone must also be unlocked to add an eSIM from another provider.

    Matches your phone's 4G and 5G bands against the bands operators use in each country.

    Copy them from the spec sheet. Plain numbers are 4G bands; prefix 5G bands with n.
