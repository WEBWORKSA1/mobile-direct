Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase or payment is necessary to enter or win. A purchase does not improve your chances.
  2. Open to individuals aged 18 or over (or the age of majority where they live), where such contests are lawful. Void where prohibited or restricted. Employees of Mobile.Direct and their households are not eligible.
  3. One entry per person per contest. Entries must be the entrant's original work and must not infringe anyone's rights.
  4. Winners are selected by a panel of judges on merit using the criteria stated for each contest. This is a contest of skill, not chance.
  5. The winner is notified by email and must reply within 14 days, or an alternate winner is selected. Prizes cannot be exchanged for cash except where required by law. Taxes are the winner's responsibility.

  7. Personal data is used only to run the contest, as described in our privacy policy.
