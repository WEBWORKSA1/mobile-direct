How the plan match works

  1. You describe your usage. Lines, data, what matters most and your current bill.
  2. We match a plan type. Budget brand, network plan, travel setup or premium unlimited.
  3. We price it locally. Using typical prices for that plan type in your country.
  4. You compare the shortlist. With direct links to each carrier's own site.

Questions

Is the plan match free?

Yes. You see your recommendation and estimated saving without entering any personal details.

Where do the price ranges come from?

From typical published prices for each plan type in your country, reviewed regularly. Always confirm the final price on the provider's site.

What happens after I request the report?

We email your shortlist and, only if you ask, a plan specialist or up to three matched providers contact you. You can opt out at any time.
