Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Effective 19 November 2026. This policy explains what Mobile.Direct collects, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Information you send us through forms: for example your name, email, phone number, postal code, quiz answers or application details. Forms are delivered to our private inbox through a form-relay service (FormSubmit).
  • Tool inputs such as data-usage sliders or IMEI numbers are processed in your browser and are not sent to us unless you submit a form.
  • Technical data collected automatically by our host (GitHub Pages) and by third-party services listed below, such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed.
  • Settings stored in your browser's local storage, such as your theme choice and that you have seen the cookie notice.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, send the report, alerts or newsletter you asked for, and run contests.
  • When you request a plan match, quotes or offers and tick the consent box, to share your request with up to three relevant providers or partners so they can contact you about it.
  • To improve the site and keep it secure.

We do not sell personal information for money. We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose, and job applications for up to 12 months.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn more at how Google uses information in advertising. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's certified consent message before personalised ads are shown.

Other third-party services

  • YouTube videos are embedded in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play.
  • Google Fonts delivers the site's typeface.
  • PayPal processes contributions; we never see your card details.
  • FormSubmit relays form messages to our inbox.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25, or California's CCPA/CPRA), you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, or opt out of sharing. Use the contact form and choose "Privacy request". We respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 (16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the effective date above.
