Deal calendar

When phone and plan prices drop during the year, so you can time your purchase. Get alerts when it is time to act.

WhenWhat usually happens
JanuaryPost-holiday clearance on last year's models; carrier promotions to win switchers.
February–MarchSamsung Galaxy S launch: pre-order bonuses and the strongest trade-in offers of the year for Galaxy owners.
April–MayMid-range launches (A-series phones); older flagships drop in price.
JulyBig online retail sale events: unlocked phones and accessories hit yearly lows.
AugustBack-to-school promotions and Pixel launches; student discounts peak.
SeptemberNew iPhones: carriers push trade-in credits; previous models fall in price. Sell your old phone before launch.
OctoberHoliday promotions start early; foldables often discounted.
Late NovemberBlack Friday and Cyber Monday: the deepest discounts on phones and plans.
DecemberHoliday bundles and, in Canada and the UK, Boxing Day sales.

Patterns from recent years. Exact dates vary by country and retailer.
