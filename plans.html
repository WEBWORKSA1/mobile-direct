Plans for every kind of user

Shortcuts for the situations people ask us about most.

Seniors

Light prepaid on the strongest local network, with Wi-Fi calling switched on. Ask about age-based discounts; several carriers offer them.

Students

Mid-size data from a sub-brand, paid monthly so you can switch at term breaks. Check student discounts and campus coverage.

Families

Multi-line plans where the per-line price falls as lines are added. Split fairly with our bill splitter.

Frequent travellers

A plan with roaming included, or a cheap home plan plus travel eSIMs.

Newcomers

Prepaid with no credit check gets you a local number fast. Bring an unlocked phone and check its bands.

Businesses

Pooled data, device management and an account manager. Request competing quotes.

Questions

Are these prices live?

No. They are typical ranges for each plan type, reviewed regularly. Promotions change often, so always confirm on the carrier's site.

What is a sub-brand?

A budget brand owned by a network operator, sold at lower prices with fewer perks. It uses the parent network's towers.
