Unlocked phones: how to check and how to unlock

Mobile.Direct editorial team. Updated 18 November 2026. 5-minute read.

Key takeaways

  • A locked phone only works with the carrier that sold it.
  • Test by inserting or adding a SIM from a different carrier.
  • Canada and the UK ban the sale of locked phones; the US relies on carrier policies.
  • Paying off a financed phone is usually required before unlocking.

Carriers sometimes lock the phones they sell so they only accept that carrier's SIM. A lock protects the carrier while the phone is being paid off. An unlocked phone accepts a SIM or eSIM from any compatible network, which lets you switch plans freely and use local SIMs when travelling.

How to check if your phone is unlocked

  • Swap test: insert a SIM from a different carrier (borrow one from a friend). If you can make a call, the phone is unlocked.
  • iPhone: go to Settings, General, About. Next to Carrier Lock (or Network Provider Lock) it says "No SIM restrictions" when unlocked.
  • Android: look for a SIM lock or network unlock section in Settings, or try adding a second carrier's eSIM.
  • Ask: your carrier can confirm the status from your IMEI.

The rules by country

CountryWhat applies
CanadaThe national Wireless Code requires phones to be sold unlocked and bans unlocking fees.
United KingdomThe regulator stopped providers selling locked phones from December 2021.
United StatesCarriers follow their own unlocking policies under industry commitments: typically once the device is paid off and the account is in good standing, or after a set period. Some unlock automatically.
IndiaMost phones are sold unlocked through retail.
AustraliaCarriers unlock on request; most phones are now sold unlocked.

Policies change, so always confirm on your carrier's own unlocking page.

How to request an unlock

  1. Pay off any device balance.
  2. Find your IMEI by dialling *#06#.
  3. Submit the carrier's online unlock form or call support.
  4. Restart the phone with the new SIM once the carrier confirms.

Unlocked does not mean compatible

An unlocked phone still needs to support the bands the new network uses. Phones built for one market may miss key low-frequency bands in another, which shows up as weak indoor signal. Use our band checker before switching or travelling.

Avoid "unlock code" scams

Paid third-party unlock services range from legitimate to fraudulent. A phone that is reported lost, stolen or unpaid can be blocked again later. Use your carrier's official process whenever you can.

Frequently asked questions

Does unlocking void my warranty?

An official carrier unlock does not affect the manufacturer's warranty.

Is a carrier lock the same as an activation lock?

No. Activation lock (Find My or Google account protection) ties the phone to the owner's account and must be removed by the owner. It is separate from carrier locking.
