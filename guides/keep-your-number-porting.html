Keep your number when switching: porting guide by country

Mobile.Direct editorial team. Updated 18 November 2026. 5-minute read.

Key takeaways

  • Do not cancel your old plan before the port completes.
  • You need your account number and usually a transfer PIN or code.
  • Most wireless ports complete within hours.
  • The new provider starts the port, not the old one.

Number porting moves your phone number from one provider to another. In nearly every country you have a legal right to keep your number, and the process is quick when you have the right details ready.

The universal rule

Start the switch with your new provider, and keep your old service active until the new SIM works. Cancelling first can release your number.

United States

  • You need your account number and a number transfer PIN, which most carriers generate in their app or online account.
  • Give these to the new carrier during sign-up.
  • Wireless-to-wireless ports usually complete within hours.

Canada

  • Have your account number and the phone's IMEI ready.
  • The new carrier requests the port; wireless ports are normally completed within a few hours.

United Kingdom

  • Text PAC to 65075 to receive your Porting Authorisation Code by text. It is valid for 30 days.
  • Give the code to your new provider; the switch usually happens the next working day.
  • To leave without keeping your number, text STAC to 75075.

India

  • SMS PORT followed by your 10-digit mobile number to 1900 to receive a Unique Porting Code.
  • Visit or contact the new operator with the code and your ID.
  • The change usually completes within a few working days.

Australia

  • Provide your account number (prepaid customers may use date of birth) to the new provider.
  • Ports often complete the same day.

If something goes wrong

  1. Check the name and account details match exactly.
  2. Confirm the PIN or code has not expired.
  3. Ask the new provider for the port status and any rejection reason.

Protect your account with a PIN so nobody else can port your number away. Number hijacking is a common route to account takeover.

Frequently asked questions

Will porting cancel my old plan?

Yes, once the port completes your old service ends automatically. You may still owe any remaining device payments or final bill.

Can I port a prepaid number?

Yes. Prepaid numbers can be ported in all the countries listed here.
