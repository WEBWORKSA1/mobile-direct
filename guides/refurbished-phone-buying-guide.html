Refurbished phones: grades, battery and what to check

Mobile.Direct editorial team. Updated 18 November 2026. 5-minute read.

Key takeaways

  • Grades describe looks, not function, on reputable marketplaces.
  • Ask for battery health of at least 85%, or a new battery.
  • Insist on a warranty of 12 months and a return window.
  • Last year's flagship refurbished often beats a new mid-range phone.

A refurbished phone has been returned, inspected, repaired where needed, cleaned and resold. Bought from a reputable seller, it works like new and usually costs 30–70% less, depending on age and condition.

How grading works

Large refurbished marketplaces grade phones by cosmetic condition. All grades must pass the same functional tests.

GradeWhat to expect
Excellent / PremiumLooks new; marks invisible with the screen on
GoodLight scratches visible up close
FairVisible scratches or scuffs on body or screen

If you use a case and screen protector, "Good" is often the best value.

Battery health matters most

Lithium-ion batteries lose capacity with charge cycles. Reputable sellers guarantee a minimum capacity (often 80%), and some offer a new battery as an option. For a phone you plan to keep two years or more, ask for 85% or higher, or budget for a battery replacement.

Warranty and returns

  • Look for at least a 12-month warranty.
  • A 14–30 day return window lets you test properly.
  • Confirm the phone is unlocked and not blocklisted.
  • Check what is in the box; many come with a cable but no charger.

What to test on day one

  1. Calls on speaker and earpiece, and the microphone.
  2. Every camera, including video and focus.
  3. Face or fingerprint unlock.
  4. Charging with a cable and wirelessly if supported.
  5. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GPS and mobile data.
  6. Battery health in settings.

Refurbished or new?

Refurbished wins when you want flagship cameras and performance on a mid-range budget. New wins when you want the longest software support, or when a launch promotion brings the new price close to refurbished. The refurbished vs new calculator shows the real saving including a battery swap.

Buying refurbished also cuts electronic waste: reusing a phone avoids most of the raw materials and emissions of manufacturing a new one.

Frequently asked questions

Is refurbished the same as used?

No. Used phones are sold as-is by their owner. Refurbished phones are tested, repaired if needed and usually come with a warranty.

How many years of updates will a refurbished phone get?

Updates depend on the model's launch date. Check the manufacturer's support policy and subtract the phone's age.
