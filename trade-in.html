    Before you sell

    1. Back up photos, messages and authenticator apps.
    2. Move your eSIM or take out the SIM.
    3. Turn off Find My (iPhone) or remove your Google account (Android).
    4. Erase all content and settings.
    5. Photograph the phone's condition before shipping.

    When to sell

    Used prices drop fastest when a new generation launches: September for iPhone, early in the year for Galaxy S. Selling two to four weeks before a launch usually beats waiting.

    Carrier trade-in promotions often pay as bill credits spread over 24–36 months. Cash from a buyback buyer is yours immediately.
